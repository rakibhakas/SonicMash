# 🎵 SonicMash

### Music. Together. Everywhere.

SonicMash is an Android music application designed to bring people closer through music and connected-device experiences.

This project explores a modern music experience with Bluetooth connectivity, collaborative listening, and social communication features. The available functionality depends on the version of the app you're using.

**Platform:** Android  
**Package format:** APK

## 📥 Download and Install

You don't need Android Studio or programming knowledge to install the APK.

### Step 1: Download SonicMash

1. Open the [SonicMash GitHub repository](https://github.com/rakibhakas/SonicMash).
2. Open the **Releases** section.
3. Select the latest release.
4. Download `SonicMash.apk` or the APK attached to that release.

If no release is available yet, download the APK from the repository's designated download location.

### Step 2: Allow installation

Android may block apps installed outside Google Play. If you trust the source of the APK, follow these steps:

1. Open the downloaded APK using your phone's Downloads app or file manager.
2. If Android displays a security warning, open the relevant settings.
3. Allow **Install unknown apps** for the browser or file manager you're using.
4. Return to the APK and tap **Install**.

The exact wording may vary by Android version and device manufacturer.

**Security note:** Only install APKs from sources you trust. Android's warning exists because apps installed outside an official store may not have undergone the same review process.

### Step 3: Open the app

After installation:

1. Tap **Open**, or find SonicMash in your app drawer.
2. Follow any instructions displayed by the app.
3. Grant permissions only when you understand why they are needed and want to use the related feature.

That's it! SonicMash is installed.

### Installation troubleshooting

**Android says the app cannot be installed**
- Check that your device meets the app's Android version requirements.
- Ensure the APK downloaded completely.
- If another version of SonicMash is already installed, a signing-key mismatch or version conflict may prevent an update.
- Avoid uninstalling an existing version if you need to preserve its local data.

**Android blocks the installation**
- Check the installation settings for your browser or file manager.
- Do not bypass security warnings if you cannot verify the APK's source.

**The app crashes or does not open**
- Restart your phone and try again.
- Download the latest available release.
- Report the issue with your Android version, device model, and steps to reproduce it.

## 🚀 Getting Started

### 1. Launch SonicMash

Open the app from your home screen or app drawer. Explore the available interface and follow any onboarding instructions shown by your installed version.

### 2. Explore the music experience

Look for the music controls and listening features available in your build. Use the controls provided by the app to access supported music functionality.

### 3. Connect compatible devices

If Bluetooth or connected-device functionality is available:

1. Enable Bluetooth on your Android phone.
2. Bring the compatible devices close to your phone.
3. Follow SonicMash's on-screen connection instructions.
4. Grant nearby-device or Bluetooth permissions if required.
5. Check the connection status before attempting a connected-device session.

Bluetooth audio routing and synchronized playback across several phones are different capabilities. Successful Bluetooth pairing alone does not guarantee synchronized playback.

### 4. Explore communication features

If group messaging, nearby-user discovery, or walkie-talkie functionality is available in your installed version, follow the relevant in-app controls and permission prompts.

Microphone access may be required for voice communication. Use these features only with people who have agreed to participate.

**Note:** Features still under development may be incomplete or unavailable in some releases.

## 🔐 Permissions and Privacy

SonicMash may request Android permissions depending on the features implemented in the installed version.

- **Bluetooth / Nearby devices:** May be required for device discovery and connections.
- **Microphone:** May be required for voice communication.
- **Notifications:** May be required for supported alerts.

Review each permission prompt before granting access. You can review or change app permissions in your Android device settings.

Do not assume that messages, voice communications, or device discovery are encrypted or fully private unless the relevant protections are documented and verified.
